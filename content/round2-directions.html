round 2 · five directions · login + dashboard only

The references are playful. The dashboard is where you look at real money.

Both of your references — the Spotted onboarding screen and the blue-star type app — are illustrative, character-driven, flat colour-blocked, warm. None of them is a dense financial instrument you open every morning. Reconciling those two facts is the entire job of this round.

So these five directions are not five palettes. They are five answers to one question: how far does the playfulness penetrate past the login screen? Every direction below shows the same two surfaces with the same real numbers — a ₹42,18,650 net worth, an HDFC alert auto-imported three minutes ago — so the only variable is the philosophy.
